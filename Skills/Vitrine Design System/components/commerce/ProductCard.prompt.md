White rounded tile with image, favorite, brand, 2-line title, rating, price and quick-add — use in grids (4-up desktop) and carousels.

```jsx
<ProductCard brand="Norte" title="Fone sem fio com cancelamento de ruído" price={349.9} original={499.9}
  rating={4.6} reviews={1283} badge="-30%" shipping="Frete grátis amanhã" onAdd={() => add(id)} />
```
